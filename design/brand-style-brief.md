# BEEF — Brand & Style Brief

**Product:** Free, ads-supported gay male social/dating app (grid-based proximity app, in the spirit of Grindr but with its own identity).
**Personality:** Campy, witty, confident, playful. Bold. A little messy, on purpose. Think drag-brunch energy crossed with a high-fashion editorial spread — never try-hard, never explicit, always a wink.

---

## 1. Name-adjacent tagline suggestions
BEEF is itself the pun (beef = meat/muscle/male + a "beef" you can pick). Taglines lean into food, muscle, and appetite — cheeky but classy.

1. **"Beef, aged to perfection."** — plays on the name's double meaning.
2. **"Meat for the main course."**
3. **"A cut above the rest."**
4. **"Get your beef on."** — short, punchy, app-like.
5. **"Well-marbled. Locally sourced."** — leans into the proximity hook.
6. **"Fresh. Local. Full of flavor."** — closest to a value-prop tagline.

> Recommended primary: **"A cut above the rest."** (confident, quotable, works as both a brand line and a jab at competitors). Alternates for subheads: "Get your beef on." / "Local. Fresh. Full of flavor."

## 2. Brand voice (campy, witty, confident, playful)
- **Saucy, not smutty.** Humor does the flirting; the copy never needs to be explicit.
- **Self-aware.** BEEF knows it's a hookup app and says so with a smirk ("No small talk required — but we won't judge you if you make some.").
- **First-person, second-person, full-bleed confidence.** Write like a handsome friend giving you a pep talk.
- **Puns are currency.** Food, butcher, gym, and "beef" double-entendres everywhere, but never forced.
- **Short sentences. Punchy rhythm.** App copy reads in taps, not paragraphs.
- **Tone checks:** witty > clever > cute. Never mean, never gatekeeping.

### Voice examples (for microcopy)
- Loading state: *"Warming up the grill…"*
- Empty grid: *"Nobody here yet. Be the first slab of beef."*
- Profile prompt: *"Your cut, your marbling — show it off."*
- Notification: *"Someone just checked out your cuts."*
- Waitlist button: *"Get on the grill."* / *"Join the waitlist."*

## 3. Color palette (hex codes)
A warm "meat-and-juice" base punched up with vibrant fashion neons and a confident red. The vibe: rich, warm, appetizing, yet unmistakably stylish.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| **Steak Red** (primary) | `#D62839` | Primary brand color: buttons, CTAs, highlights, name/brand mark |
| **Beef Brown** (secondary) | `#6F2A1E` | Deep accents, footer, hover depth, warm shadows |
| **Char** (ink / text) | `#1E1B18` | Primary text, near-black warm ink |
| **Cream** (background) | `#FBF3E8` | Page/background base — warm off-white, never cold |
| **Sizzle Orange** (accent) | `#F77F00` | Warm accent: tags, badges, heat/waitlist highlights |
| **Margarita Lime** (accent) | `#A4C639` | Fresh contrast accent (proximity "local freshness") |
| **Berry Punch** (accent) | `#B3146B` | Campy accent for playful UI moments, gradients with red |

**Gradient hero suggestion:** `#D62839 → #F77F00 → #B3146B` (steak red smoking into sizzle orange, punched with berry) — instantly recognizable, warm, and glamorous. Reserve standalone red for primary actions so the brand mark stays the anchor.

## 4. Typography direction (fashionable fonts)
Bold, confident display type paired with a clean, highly legible body font. A fashion-magazine sense of scale: huge display, tight letter-spacing on the mark.

- **Display / Headline:** **Clash Display** (Fontshare) — the flagship. Geometric, punchy, fashion-forward, free for commercial use. Pairs muscle with elegance.
  - *Backup:* **Unbounded** (Google Fonts, expandable display) or **Space Grotesk** at heavy weights.
- **Secondary / Accent (campy flair):** **Pacifico** or **Caveat** only for a hand-scribbled wink-word ("fresh", "local") — used sparingly, never for body.
- **Body / UI:** **Inter** (clean, neutral, excellent legibility) *or* **Space Grotesk** for a slightly more characterful feel.
- **Brand mark "BEEF":** extr-boldt extended letterspaced uppercase in Clash Display / Unbounded, set tight against the Steak Red.

> If only Google Fonts are available (easier deployment), use **Unbounded** (display) + **Space Grotesk** (body) — both on Google Fonts with free commercial license.

## 6. Imagery direction
- Athletic, attractive male models — tasteful/cheeky at most, never nude or explicit.
- Roughly **~75% white / ~25% other** backgrounds across sets (see generated assets).
- Saturated, vibrant color grading (magenta, coral, emerald, cobalt, neon) on warm/urban set pieces.
- Editorial high-fashion lighting; confident poses, playful energy, not smoldering nudes.
- Consistent styling: bold color-blocking, graphic sunglasses, open shirts, fitted athletic wear.
